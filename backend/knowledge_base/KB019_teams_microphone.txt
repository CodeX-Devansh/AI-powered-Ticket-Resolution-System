Article ID: KB019
Title: Teams Microphone Not Working
Category: Collaboration
Subcategory: Audio

Problem:
The employee's microphone does not work during an online meeting.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Confirm the microphone is not muted.
2. Check the selected microphone in meeting settings.
3. Test the microphone using the operating-system audio settings.
4. Disconnect and reconnect an external headset if used.
5. Restart the collaboration application.
6. Verify application microphone permissions.

Resolution:
Perform a test call and confirm that the microphone is detected.

Escalation:
Escalate to Desktop Support if the microphone does not work in other applications.

Priority Guidance:
Medium.

Keywords:
Teams, microphone, audio, headset, meeting audio
