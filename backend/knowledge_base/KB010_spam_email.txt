Article ID: KB010
Title: Spam and Unwanted Email
Category: Email
Subcategory: Spam

Problem:
The employee receives unwanted or suspicious bulk email.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Do not click unknown links or open unexpected attachments.
2. Mark unwanted messages as spam using the approved mail client.
3. Do not reply to suspicious senders.
4. Report suspected phishing using the approved reporting mechanism.

Resolution:
The message is reported and removed according to company policy.

Escalation:
Escalate suspected phishing or credential-harvesting messages to Security.

Priority Guidance:
High when the message appears to be a targeted security threat; otherwise Low or Medium.

Keywords:
spam, phishing, suspicious email, unwanted email
