Article ID: KB030
Title: Suspected Data Loss or Unauthorized Data Exposure
Category: Security
Subcategory: Data Loss

Problem:
An employee believes confidential company information may have been sent to an unauthorized recipient or exposed.

Immediate Actions:
1. Do not attempt to conceal or delete evidence.
2. Record what information may have been exposed.
3. Record the recipient, system, or location involved.
4. Report the incident immediately through the approved Security or Data Protection process.
5. Follow instructions from the incident-response team.

Resolution:
Security and the appropriate data-protection team investigate and determine containment and notification requirements.

Escalation:
Always escalate immediately to Security/Data Protection.

Priority Guidance:
Critical.

Keywords:
data loss, data exposure, confidential information, unauthorized sharing, security incident
