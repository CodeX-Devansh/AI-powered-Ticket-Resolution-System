Article ID: KB007
Title: Outlook Not Working
Category: Email
Subcategory: Outlook

Problem:
The employee's Outlook desktop application does not open, sync, or display current mail.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Confirm internet connectivity.
2. Check whether Outlook Web Access works.
3. Restart Outlook.
4. Restart the workstation if necessary.
5. Confirm that Outlook is not in offline mode.
6. Check whether the mailbox is near its storage limit.
7. If the problem is limited to the desktop application, escalate for profile repair.

Resolution:
Verify that Outlook opens and synchronizes new messages.

Escalation:
Escalate to Email Support if Outlook Web Access also fails or mailbox service is unavailable.

Priority Guidance:
High when email service is unavailable for multiple employees; otherwise Medium.

Keywords:
Outlook, email client, mailbox, email sync
