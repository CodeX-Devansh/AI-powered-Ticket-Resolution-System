Article ID: KB014
Title: Laptop Does Not Start
Category: Hardware
Subcategory: Power

Problem:
The employee's laptop does not power on.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Connect the approved power adapter.
2. Confirm the power indicator is active.
3. Try a known-good power outlet.
4. Disconnect nonessential peripherals.
5. Hold the power button according to the approved device procedure.
6. Do not open the device chassis.

Resolution:
If the laptop starts normally, verify the operating system loads successfully.

Escalation:
Escalate immediately to Desktop Support if there is no power indication or suspected hardware failure.

Priority Guidance:
High when the employee cannot work and no alternate workstation is available.

Keywords:
laptop not starting, computer not turning on, power issue
