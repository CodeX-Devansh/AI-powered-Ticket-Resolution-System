Article ID: KB005
Title: Account Locked
Category: Account
Subcategory: Account Lock

Problem:
The employee's corporate account is locked after repeated unsuccessful authentication attempts.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Stop attempting to sign in repeatedly.
2. Confirm the employee ID.
3. Use the approved account-unlock procedure if available.
4. Reset the password if the password may be incorrect.
5. Wait for the standard lockout period when automatic unlock is configured.

Resolution:
Verify that the employee can sign in successfully after the account is unlocked.

Escalation:
Escalate to Identity and Access Management when the account remains locked or the unlock process fails.

Priority Guidance:
High when the employee requires immediate access to critical work; otherwise Medium.

Keywords:
account locked, locked account, login failed, authentication
