Article ID: KB008
Title: Email Not Received
Category: Email
Subcategory: Mail Delivery

Problem:
An expected email has not appeared in the employee's mailbox.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Search the mailbox using the sender and subject.
2. Check Junk or Spam folders.
3. Check mailbox rules and filters.
4. Confirm the sender used the correct address.
5. Ask the sender to verify whether delivery failed.
6. Check whether other messages are arriving normally.

Resolution:
Locate the message or confirm successful delivery.

Escalation:
Escalate to Email Support if multiple recipients are affected or delivery failures are reported.

Priority Guidance:
Medium unless the missing message blocks a critical business process.

Keywords:
email missing, email not received, mail delivery, mailbox
