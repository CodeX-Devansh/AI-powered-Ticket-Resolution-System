Article ID: KB001
Title: VPN Troubleshooting
Category: Network
Subcategory: VPN

Problem:
The employee can connect to the corporate VPN, but internal company applications or websites cannot be accessed.

Symptoms:
- VPN shows connected.
- Public internet may work.
- Internal applications fail to open.
- Internal websites may be unreachable.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Confirm that normal internet access is working.
2. Disconnect from the VPN.
3. Close and restart the VPN client.
4. Clear the local DNS cache.
5. Reconnect to the VPN.
6. Retry the internal application.
7. If possible, check whether other employees have the same problem.

Resolution:
If the VPN reconnects successfully and internal resources are accessible, no further action is required.

Escalation:
Escalate to Network Support if the issue continues or multiple employees are affected.

Priority Guidance:
High when multiple employees are affected; Medium for a single employee.

Keywords:
VPN, remote access, internal application, corporate network, VPN connected
