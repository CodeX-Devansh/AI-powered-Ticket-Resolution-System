Article ID: KB021
Title: Shared Drive Access
Category: Access
Subcategory: Shared Drive

Problem:
An employee cannot access a shared company drive.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Confirm the employee is connected to the corporate network or VPN when required.
2. Verify the shared-drive path.
3. Check whether other employees can access the same location.
4. Confirm the employee's account is active.
5. Verify that the employee has been granted the required access.

Resolution:
Access should be restored after connectivity and permissions are confirmed.

Escalation:
Escalate permission issues to the appropriate Access Management or application owner.

Priority Guidance:
High when a critical team share is unavailable to multiple users; otherwise Medium.

Keywords:
shared drive, network drive, file share, drive access
