Article ID: KB002
Title: Corporate Wi-Fi Connection Troubleshooting
Category: Network
Subcategory: Wi-Fi

Problem:
The employee cannot connect to the corporate wireless network.

Symptoms:
- Corporate Wi-Fi is not visible.
- Wi-Fi authentication fails.
- Device connects and immediately disconnects.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled on the device.
2. Move closer to the access point if the signal is weak.
3. Forget the corporate Wi-Fi network.
4. Reconnect and enter the corporate credentials.
5. Restart the device.
6. Check whether another device can connect to the same network.

Resolution:
Reconnect successfully to the corporate Wi-Fi and verify internet access.

Escalation:
Escalate to Network Support if several employees are affected or authentication continues to fail.

Priority Guidance:
Medium for one employee; High when an office area is affected.

Keywords:
Wi-Fi, wireless, network, connection, corporate Wi-Fi
