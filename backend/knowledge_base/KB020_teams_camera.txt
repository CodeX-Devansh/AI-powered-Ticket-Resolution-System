Article ID: KB020
Title: Teams Camera Not Working
Category: Collaboration
Subcategory: Video

Problem:
The employee's camera does not work during an online meeting.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Confirm the camera shutter or privacy control is open.
2. Verify the correct camera is selected.
3. Check application camera permissions.
4. Close other applications that may be using the camera.
5. Restart the collaboration application.
6. Test the camera in the operating-system camera application.

Resolution:
The camera should display a live preview and work in a test meeting.

Escalation:
Escalate to Desktop Support if the camera fails in multiple applications.

Priority Guidance:
Medium.

Keywords:
Teams, camera, video, webcam, meeting video
