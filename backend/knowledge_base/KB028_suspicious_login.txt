Article ID: KB028
Title: Suspicious Login Activity
Category: Security
Subcategory: Suspicious Login

Problem:
An employee receives an alert for a login they do not recognize.

Immediate Actions:
1. Do not approve an unexpected MFA request.
2. Do not click links in suspicious security-alert emails.
3. Report the event through the approved security reporting process.
4. If the employee believes credentials may have been compromised, follow the approved password-reset procedure.
5. Provide the security team with the alert time and location shown in the alert.

Resolution:
Security validates the event and secures the account if compromise is suspected.

Escalation:
Always escalate suspected unauthorized access to Security.

Priority Guidance:
Critical until Security determines that the event is benign.

Keywords:
suspicious login, unauthorized login, account compromise, security alert
